The idea

Your libraries.
Your rules.

Conditions organizes photos, audio, and documents into libraries that become available when their conditions are met. Every saved item also carries a snapshot of its available context.

Then we turn those snapshots into something you can explore: a web of content, connected by similarity.

A small app.

Set the conditions.

Create libraries and combine conditions with AND, OR, NOT, and parentheses. This conditional logic determines what is available right now. Open a library to browse, search, and swipe through its content.

Under the surface

A little math.
A lot of decisions.

Midnight is not an edge.

11:55 PM and 12:05 AM are ten minutes apart. Modular arithmetic lets daily time wrap around instead of treating those moments as opposite ends of a line.

double minutes = Math.abs(an - bn) % 1440;
difference = Math.min(minutes, 1440 - minutes) / 720;
From NeighborMetric.java

Different units. Shared space.

Battery, temperature, and timestamps live on different scales. Normalization brings numeric values into a common range; weights let us choose what matters.

normalized = Double.isFinite(range) ? (number - min) / range
        : (number / 2 - min / 2) / (max / 2 - min / 2);
From ContentWebPlot.java

Similarity becomes a graph.

Networks, device sets, and weather categories have no natural smaller-to-larger order. We rank category matches first, then weighted numeric distance. Each item chooses its nearest neighbors, and their connections form an undirected graph.

int order = Integer.compare(matches(item, b), matches(item, a));
return order != 0 ? order : Double.compare(distance(item, a), distance(item, b));
From NeighborMetric.java

Random, but repeatable.

Sampling helps keep a crowded plot readable, and randomness breaks exact neighbor ties. Reusing a seed keeps those choices consistent when the figure redraws. Reshuffle changes the seed when we want another sample.

java.util.Collections.shuffle(candidates,
        new java.util.Random(seed ^ Long.parseLong(item.id)));
